Simple Steps to Trade

Trader Process

  1. Browse & ChooseSearch by game or item and find the listing you want.
  2. Make an OfferTell the owner what you'll give in exchange, and chat to settle the details.
  3. Owner AcceptsThe owner can accept, decline, or send a counter-offer.
  4. Confirm & RateBoth sides confirm the trade, then leave a rating.

Poster Process

  1. Post a TradeAdd photos, a description, and what you're looking for. Listing is free.
  2. Review OffersOffers arrive in your inbox. Chat with offerers before deciding.
  3. Complete the TradeOnce both of you confirm, the listing is marked sold.
Learn more about GameBarterHub
What is GameBarterHub?

A community marketplace where players trade in-game items, accounts, and resources with each other, instead of paying cash.

Is it safe to trade here?

Every trade needs both sides to confirm, chats and offers stay on the platform, and traders can earn verified badges and ratings. Never share your password outside a trade you've agreed to, and open a support ticket if something looks off.

How do I post a trade?

Create an account, tap Post, pick the game, add photos and tell people what you're looking for in return.

What can I trade?

In-game items, accounts, and resources across the games listed on the site. Pick the matching game when you post.

Is GameBarterHub free?

Yes. Creating an account and posting trades doesn't cost anything.
